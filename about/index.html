Duality

An interactive piece of art to play with particle-wave duality

The idea

Is light a wave or a particle? Quantum physics answers: both, depending on how you look at it. Duality takes this riddle as a playground. A field of waves and a swarm of particles share the same canvas, and each one keeps shaping the other: particles throw energy into the field, and the ripples they create steer the particles in return.

The piece borrows its inspiration from walking droplets: oil droplets bouncing on a vibrating bath, guided by the very waves they emit. In 2005, Yves Couder and Emmanuel Fort showed that these macroscopic objects reproduce some strikingly quantum-like behaviors, such as diffraction and interference through slits.

The field

The background is a grid holding a scalar field c(x, y, t). It obeys a damped, non-linear wave equation:

τ ∂²c/∂t² = D Δc − ∂c/∂t − ν₁ c − ν₃ c³

  • D sets how fast and far waves propagate (diffusion),
  • τ is the characteristic time of the field,
  • ν₁ is a viscosity pulling the field back to rest,
  • ν₃ damps large amplitudes and keeps the field from exploding.

Walls are cells where the field is held at zero, which makes waves reflect and diffract.

The particles

Each particle has a position r, an orientation θ and an internal energy u. They behave like active Brownian particles:

dr/dt = v₀ e(θ) − µ ∇c + √(2Dp) ξ

dθ/dt = ω (cos θ ∂yc − sin θ ∂xc) + √(2Dr) ξ

du/dt = 1/τu + λ |c|

  • v₀ is a self-propulsion speed along the orientation,
  • µ makes particles slide down the field gradient,
  • ω makes them turn according to the gradient,
  • ξ is random noise (Brownian motion, in position and rotation).

Energy builds up over time, and faster where the field is strong. Once it crosses a threshold, the particle releases it into the field around it, creating a new ripple. This is the bounce of the droplet: the particle writes the wave, the wave guides the particle.

The sound

The field is also an instrument. Depending on the mode, the grid is read as a raw audio signal, analysed through its frequency spectrum, or voiced by the particles themselves. What you hear is the same matter as what you see.

The modes

The Playground
Two thousand particles and a field to stir with your pointer. Tune energy injection and diffusion.
The Tempest
Raindrops fall at random on the surface. Play with their intensity and frequency.
The Constellations
A dense swarm in which particles draw links to nearby partners, like stars in the night sky.
The Bouncing Droplets
A handful of self-propelled droplets, each surfing on its own wave and on the waves of the others.
The Interferences
A double-slit experiment: waves pass through two openings and interfere on the other side.
The Poem
Words are written into the field, letter by letter, and particles gather around their shapes.

The art

Duality is not a scientific simulation in the strict sense. The equations are real, but the parameters are chosen for what they show and what they sound like. It is an invitation to feel with your hands and ears a duality that physics usually describes with abstract mathematics.

Have fun interacting, and be careful with your precious ears: keep the sound low.

Alex Andrix
